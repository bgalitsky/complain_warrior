What Complaint Warrior does

Complaint Warrior helps consumers describe a problem with a business, prepare complaint letters and follow-ups, track responses, and organize materials for possible escalation. Optional tools assist with calls, chargebacks, regulatory complaints, and small-claims preparation.

Why connect Gmail?

With your permission, Complaint Warrior reads complaint-related correspondence, sends messages and follow-ups you authorize, and uses labels to track email workflows. AI features may process relevant correspondence to help prepare drafts and summarize responses. Gmail access can be revoked at any time through your Google Account.
