Privacy Policy

Complaint Warrior helps you organize consumer complaints, prepare communications, and track responses. Connecting Gmail lets the service handle complaint-related email on your behalf. This policy explains the data involved and your choices.

1. Who operates the service

Complaint Warrior is operated by Boris Galitsky. For privacy questions, access requests, or deletion requests, contact bgalitsky@hotmail.com. This policy covers the Complaint Warrior application and its related complaint-resolution tools.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you provide: contact details, complaint narratives, business names, purchase or transaction details, correspondence, evidence files, and instructions you submit.
  • Google account information: your Google account email address and account identifier, granted permissions, and access and refresh tokens. Google handles your Google password; we do not receive it.
  • Gmail information: message and thread identifiers, sender and recipient addresses, subjects, message text, dates, labels, and relevant message content used in complaint workflows. Evidence you upload may also contain personal information about other people.
  • Activity and technical information: complaint status, generated drafts, communications and call results, and service logs that may contain timestamps, IP addresses, browser information, identifiers, and errors.

3. Why we request Gmail permissions

PermissionPurpose
openid and userinfo.emailIdentify the connected Google account and associate it with the correct complaint workspace.
gmail.readonlyRead complaint-related correspondence and detect replies.
gmail.sendSend complaint messages and follow-ups under your instructions or enabled workflow settings.
gmail.modifyRead and send complaint correspondence and manage labels or message state used to track it. This permission is broader than read-only access and also covers reading and sending.

These Gmail permissions can technically provide access beyond an individual complaint thread. We use Gmail data for the complaint-related features you enable, not for unrelated mailbox profiling. Offline access allows authorized workflows to continue when you are not actively using the application. Disconnect Gmail if you no longer want this access.

4. How we use information

We use information to organize cases, identify relevant correspondence, draft and send authorized communications, track replies, prepare evidence and escalation materials, provide optional phone assistance, troubleshoot problems, and protect the service. Automated follow-ups may send communications according to the workflow you enable. Review recipients, evidence, and settings before enabling those actions.

5. AI processing and service providers

AI-assisted features may send complaint details and relevant correspondence, including Gmail-derived text, to OpenAI for summarization, drafting, classification, and response preparation. This processing supports the complaint feature you use. Google provides account authorization and Gmail access; Amazon Web Services hosts the application. Optional calling features use Twilio and may process telephone numbers, call metadata, and, where recording or transcription is enabled, audio and transcripts. A configured ngrok tunnel may carry application traffic while that hosting arrangement is in use.

We limit service-provider access to the data needed for these functions. Complaint Warrior does not use Google user data to train or improve generalized AI or machine-learning models, and does not authorize providers to do so. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or provide it to data brokers. Gmail processing by AI providers must remain limited to the user-facing features described here.

6. Other disclosures and human access

When you authorize a communication, filing, or publication, the selected business, agency, court, platform, or other recipient receives the information included in it. Public submissions may become public records. Recipient handling is outside our control. We may also disclose information where necessary for security or applicable legal obligations. Any transfer of Google user data in a business ownership change requires prior explicit consent where Google's policies require it.

Human access to Google user data is limited to your affirmative permission to review specific data, necessary security investigations, legal requirements, or other narrowly permitted uses under Google's Limited Use rules. Access is not granted for unrelated marketing or research.

7. Storage, retention, and security

The service stores complaint records, relevant correspondence, OAuth credentials, and uploaded evidence on its hosting infrastructure. We retain information while it is needed for your complaint workflows, support, security, or applicable legal obligations. There is no fixed automatic deletion interval for all complaint records. Service providers may retain operational records under their applicable service arrangements. Data may be processed outside your country.

We restrict access to service data and use HTTPS for public connections. No service can guarantee absolute security. Do not include passwords, full payment-card numbers, or unrelated sensitive records in your complaint. Contact us if you suspect unauthorized access.

8. Disconnecting Gmail and deleting data

You can revoke access at Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized Google access but does not automatically erase copies already stored by Complaint Warrior.

The in-app complaint deletion feature removes the complaint and associated database records covered by that operation. It does not automatically delete messages from Gmail, uploaded evidence files, generated local documents, all provider-held call records, or your Gmail authorization credentials. To request deletion of those service-held records or your account data, email bgalitsky@hotmail.com from the relevant account and identify your request. Do not email passwords or tokens. We may verify ownership before acting. We will explain any legal retention exception; backup copies, where present, may persist until rotated and should not be restored to active use after deletion.

Deletion cannot retract information already delivered to recipients or remove public filings. You can separately manage messages in Gmail. Subject to applicable law, you may also request access, correction, a copy of your data, or restrictions on processing.

9. Cookies and children

The application uses session cookies or similar session state for sign-in, OAuth security, and application operation. These static policy pages contain no analytics scripts or tracking pixels. The service is intended for adults aged 18 or older and is not directed to children.

10. Google API Limited Use

Complaint Warrior's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements, and applicable Google Workspace API policies.

11. Changes and contact

We will post updates here with a revised date. If we introduce a new use of Google user data, we will provide notice and obtain consent before that new use where required. Questions and privacy requests: bgalitsky@hotmail.com.
